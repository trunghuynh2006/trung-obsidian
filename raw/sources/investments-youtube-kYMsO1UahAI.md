In this video, investor Tom Nash outlines his strategy for capitalizing on the artificial intelligence mega trend by focusing on "facilitator" companies rather than speculative applications. Drawing a parallel to the historic Gold Rush, he argues that the greatest wealth is generated by the sellers of "picks and shovels"—the infrastructure and tools that enable the industry to function.

Nash defines eight essential layers of AI infrastructure, identifying top-tier companies within each category:

Physical Infrastructure (Cloud/Data Centers): Google, Amazon (AWS), Microsoft (Azure), and Oracle (6:15-8:08).
Compute Layer (Engines/GPUs): Nvidia, AMD, Micron, ASML, and TSMC (8:09-10:09).
Networking: Arista Networks (10:10-11:09).
Power and Cooling: Constellation Energy (CEG), Bloom Energy, and Vertiv (VRT) (11:10-12:29).
Control Layer (OS for AI): Palantir (12:30-13:42).
Data Layer: MongoDB and Oracle (13:43-14:07).
Observation Layer: Datadog (14:08-14:31).
Security Layer: Crowdstrike and Zscaler (14:32-15:09).
Key Takeaways:

Long-term perspective: Nash emphasizes that investors should ignore short-term market noise and news cycles in favor of holding quality companies tied to secular trends (1:16-1:37).
Systematic investing: He stresses that success is not about chasing stock picks but about building a disciplined system and portfolio based on conviction and deep research (15:10-15:57).
Free Resource: As a gesture to his audience, he provides a link to his list of "15 Top Picks for the Next Decade" (16:58-17:28).

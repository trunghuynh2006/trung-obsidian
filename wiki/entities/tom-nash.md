# Tom Nash

**Type:** person
**Also known as:** —

## Overview

Tom Nash is an investor and YouTube educator known for applying a systematic, conviction-based approach to equity investing. His public thesis centers on identifying secular mega trends — particularly the AI infrastructure wave — and building concentrated exposure to the infrastructure layer ("picks and shovels") rather than speculative application companies. He emphasizes long-term holding, deep individual research, and treating portfolio construction as a repeatable system rather than a collection of tips.

## Key Facts

- Advocates the picks-and-shovels / facilitator investing thesis for the AI era [[wiki/sources/tom-nash-ai-infrastructure-investing]]
- Organizes AI infrastructure across eight structural layers (compute, networking, power, data, observability, security, etc.) [[wiki/sources/tom-nash-ai-infrastructure-investing]]
- Explicitly argues that short-term market noise should be ignored in favor of secular trend conviction [[wiki/sources/tom-nash-ai-infrastructure-investing]]

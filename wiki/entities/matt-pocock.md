# Matt Pocock

**Type:** person
**Also known as:** —

## Overview

Matt Pocock is a software educator known primarily for TypeScript content. In the AI era he has expanded into broader software engineering advocacy, arguing that classical fundamentals (DDD, TDD, module design) become *more* important as AI coding tools become more capable — not less. His core thesis is that AI coding agents are good tactical programmers but poor strategic architects, and that the human developer's irreplaceable role is defining module boundaries, naming concepts, and maintaining system structure.

## Key Facts

- Coined or popularized the "grill me" technique for aligning on design concept with AI before writing code [[wiki/sources/software-fundamentals-matter-more-than-ever]]
- Advocates TDD as a speed limit mechanism for AI code generation, not just a testing practice [[wiki/sources/software-fundamentals-matter-more-than-ever]]
- Applies John Ousterhout's deep module concept as an AI delegation boundary [[wiki/sources/software-fundamentals-matter-more-than-ever]]
- Frames the human-AI division of labor as: AI = tactical programmer; human = strategic architect [[wiki/sources/software-fundamentals-matter-more-than-ever]]
